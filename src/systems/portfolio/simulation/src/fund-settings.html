Fee Structure

Configure the fee structure for your fund, including management fees, performance fees, and loan-specific fees.

2.00%

Annual fee charged by the GP to cover operating expenses, typically 1-2% of committed capital.

info Formula: Management Fee = Fund Size × Management Fee Rate
Example: $100M fund with 2% management fee = $2M annual fee
20.00%

Percentage of profits above the hurdle rate that goes to the GP, typically 20%.

info Formula: Carried Interest = (Profit - Hurdle Amount) × Performance Fee Rate
Example: With 20% carry and $10M profit above hurdle, GP receives $2M
6.00%

Minimum return that must be achieved before performance fees apply, typically 6-8%.

info Formula: Hurdle Amount = LP Investment × ((1 + Hurdle Rate)^Term - 1)
Example: $100M with 8% hurdle over 10 years = $115.9M hurdle amount
3.00%

One-time fee charged to homeowners at loan origination, typically 2-3%.

info Formula: Origination Fee = Loan Amount × Origination Fee Rate
Example: $250K loan with 3% fee = $7,500 origination fee
5.00%

Annual interest rate charged on loans, typically 4-6%.

info Formula: Interest = Principal × Rate × Term
Example: $250K loan at 5% for 10 years = $125K total interest
5.00%

Percentage of fund size invested by the General Partner, typically 1-10%.

info Formula: GP Investment = Fund Size × GP Investment Percentage
Example: $100M fund with 5% GP investment = $5M GP investment

Waterfall Structure: The model uses a standard waterfall structure with:

  1. Return of capital to LPs
  2. Preferred return to LPs (hurdle rate)
  3. Catch-up provision for GP (if applicable)
  4. Carried interest split on remaining profits

Custom Capital Call Schedule

Define when additional capital will be called from investors. Dates are in months from fund inception.

Capital Call Impact: The timing of capital calls affects fund performance metrics:

  • IRR improves with delayed capital calls as investments have less time to generate returns
  • Management fees may be calculated on committed capital or invested capital
  • Deployment timing affects the fund's J-curve and time to positive returns

Early Exit Parameters

Configure the probability and timing of early loan exits.

10.00%

Chance of early loan repayment before the full term.

info Impact: Higher values lead to more reinvestments and potentially higher returns, but also more transaction costs.
Example: With 10% probability, approximately 40 out of 400 loans will exit early.

The average year when early exits occur.

info Distribution: Early exits follow a normal distribution around this average.
Example: With average year 5, most early exits will occur between years 3-7.

Controls the spread of early exits around the average.

info Impact: Higher values create more sporadic/dispersed exit patterns.
Example: With std dev of 1.5, ~68% of early exits will occur within ±1.5 years of the average.

The year after which no reinvestments will be made. Early exits before this year will be reinvested.

info Impact: This extends the fund's timeline. If set to year 5 with a 10-year fund term, the last reinvestments could extend to year 15.
Example: With cap year 5, exits in years 3-4 are reinvested for new 10-year loans.
